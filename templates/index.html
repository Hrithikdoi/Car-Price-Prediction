How it works

From form to estimate in four steps.

  1. Enter detailsBrand, model, fuel, transmission, year, engine size, mileage, doors and owners.
  2. PreprocessBrand and model use mean target encoding. Fuel and transmission are one-hot encoded.
  3. Model evaluatesA tuned CatBoost regressor scores the encoded inputs.
  4. Estimate returnedThe predicted price is shown with a summary of your inputs.

The model

Six algorithms were compared: Linear Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, SVR, XGBoost and CatBoost. The tuned CatBoost model is the one used here.
